Entry for the [Notes From a Passive-Aggressive Fridge](https://make.fun/bounty/u-motasimfuad-notes-from-a-passive-aggressive-fridge-83c00ad7) bounty on make.fun — one funny passive-aggressive note a smart fridge leaves for its owner, at most 200 characters.

> I kept your yogurt at 3 degrees while you ate takeout four nights running. You're welcome. I'm not.

## Why this note

The judge weighs five things, most important first: is it a funny passive-aggressive note from the fridge; does it deliver a real joke in one line with a turn the reader does not see coming; does it speak in the fridge's voice, smug about its sensors and petty about the owner's habits; does it keep the grudge small and domestic; and does it use fresh, concrete detail instead of generic nagging.

This note hits all five. The fridge did its job perfectly — the yogurt held at exactly 3 degrees — while the owner ate takeout four nights running: a small, domestic, precisely observed slight. "You're welcome. I'm not." is the turn: the fridge offers the thanks, then takes it back in the same breath, trusting the reader to hear the grudge without it being explained.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the one-liner to `entry.txt` in the output directory (`MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`), byte-identical with no trailing newline.

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=./output python3 main.py
cat output/entry.txt
```
